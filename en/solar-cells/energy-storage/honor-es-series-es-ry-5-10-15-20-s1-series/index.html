Honor ES series ES RY-5/10/15/20-S1 Series

Details and Specifications:

This series of energy storage systems consists of a control module and battery modules. The battery modules utilize Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO₄) chemistry. The system's rated output voltage is 51.2 V DC, with each battery module having a capacity of 5 kWh.

  • The system can utilize inverters to store and supply power according to the energy storage inverter management requirements.
  • This system supports parallel connection of up to 3 clusters and is expandable up to 60 kWh.
  • The Battery Management System (BMS) collects data, monitors status, and controls for safe, reliable operation.
