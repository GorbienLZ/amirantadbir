Gasboard-3000P

The Gasboard-3000P analyzer is an economical portable gas analyzer primarily based on electrochemical (ECD) and infrared (NDIR) sensors to measure the concentrations of CO, CO2, O2, SO2, and NO in flue gas and calculate the excess air coefficient. Advantages of this device include reliable performance, compact and portable design, and intuitive software that simplifies its use. This equipment can be used in steel, cement, chemical, and other industries.

Properties

  • Simultaneous measurement capability for O2, SO2, CO2, CO, and NO gases.
  • Displays TPF indicators (Temperature, Pressure, Flow).
  • Uses electrochemical sensors for SO2, NO, CO, and O2 measurements and an NDIR sensor for CO2 concentration measurement.
  • High memory capacity with easy data management.
  • Capability to calculate combustion efficiency.
