Gasboard-3001

The Gasboard-3001 trace gas analyzer uses Tunable Diode Laser Absorption Spectroscopy (TDLAS) technology to measure CH4 and N2O, and infrared detection (NDIR) technology to measure CO2. The portable device facilitates data collection at various locations and altitudes. It is primarily used for soil carbon flux monitoring, forest carbon flux monitoring, greenhouse gas emission monitoring, air quality monitoring, urban pollutant emission monitoring, fixed-source emission monitoring, and environmental and agricultural research.

Properties

  • Capability to measure at very low ranges with a detection limit down to 1 ppb (part per billion).
  • Reliable performance and long service life.
  • Connectivity with various communication methods.
  • Intelligent data management system, supports storage of diagnostic data for up to 3 months.
  • Fast response time in a few seconds to provide measured gas concentrations.
  • Low power consumption.
  • Continuous monitoring capability for over 8 hours.
  • Equipped with a waterproof and dustproof enclosure.
