Gasboard-3600UV

Online flue gas analyzer: The high‑temperature UV flue gas analyzer uses Ultraviolet Differential Optical Absorption Spectroscopy (UV‑DOAS) combined with hot & wet sampling technology to enable direct flue gas measurement at high temperatures. This device can simultaneously measure SO₂, NO, and NO₂ concentrations (additional gases can be added upon request) in emissions from fixed pollution sources. Compared to cold & dry analyzers, this device requires no condensation equipment, effectively preventing SO₂ and other acidic gas condensation losses and providing higher measurement accuracy. The flue gas can be analyzed via a simple flow path, reducing device failure rates and saving maintenance costs when using the Gasboard‑3600UV.

Properties

  • Hot & wet method: Flue gas is directly analyzed inside the analyzer via a heated sampling line without any condensate loss of SO₂ or other components.
  • Low detection limit: long optical path with multiple reflections, suitable for monitoring very low‑level emissions.
  • Optical method: direct measurement of NO and NO₂ without need for an NOx converter.
  • Good stability: optical path has no mechanical moving parts.
  • Resistant to strong vibrations.
  • Easy maintenance: measurement chamber is easy to clean and maintain.
  • Long service life: pulsed xenon lamp source with good stability, over 5 years of useful life.
