Gasboard-3000Plus

The Gasboard-3000Plus is an online infrared flue gas analyzer using Micro‑flow NDIR technology to simultaneously measure pollutant gas concentrations of SO2, NO, CO, CO2, and CH4. This analyzer holds a PCT patent (PCT/CN2018100767). Furthermore, it can simultaneously employ NDUV technology for direct NO2 measurement, enabling optical measurement of NO and NO2 at the same time. This analyzer can be used to measure and monitor emissions from boilers, power plants, cement factories, and incinerators. Additionally, by measuring these pollutants, it facilitates monitoring of industrial furnace combustion efficiency and process safety.

Properties

  • PCT patent approval for low‑concentration pollutant measurement using Micro‑flow NDIR technology.
  • US authorization No.: US11079322.
  • High accuracy and long service life.
  • Estimated service life of approximately 6–8 years under typical operating and maintenance conditions.
  • Capability to measure low concentrations of SO2, NO, NO2, CO, CO2, and CH4.
  • Combined with NDUV technology, direct NO2 measurement is enabled, allowing optical NOx measurement without an NOx converter.
  • No cross‑interference among measured gases.
  • Unaffected by sampling flow rate fluctuations.
  • Accurate low‑concentration gas measurement up to 200 ppm.
  • Sensor self‑diagnostic capability.
