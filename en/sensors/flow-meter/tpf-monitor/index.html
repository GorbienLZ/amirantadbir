TPF Monitor

Pitot tube flowmeter, pressure gauge, and thermometer: Operating principle: According to the Pitot tube measurement principles and based on Bernoulli's equation, the energy in the fluid flow process includes potential energy, kinetic energy, and static pressure energy. For gas flow, changes in potential energy are usually negligible, and kinetic energy and static pressure energy can be converted under certain conditions. Two Pitot tube sampling heads respectively provide total energy and static pressure energy. The difference between these two energies is called dynamic pressure. Dynamic pressure is proportional to the square of the gas flow velocity, therefore the Pitot tube can measure gas flow velocity.
