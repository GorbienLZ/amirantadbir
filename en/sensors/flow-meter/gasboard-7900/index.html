Gasboard-7900

Gasboard-7900 is a high-temperature ultrasonic flowmeter. Based on the TOF (Time of Flight) principle of ultrasonic waves, one or more pairs of ultrasonic transmitters and receivers are installed and the gas flow rate in the flue is calculated by the time difference of ultrasonic wave transmission, and the flue gas volumetric flow rate is calculated according to the cross-sectional area of the pipe. This flowmeter uses a non-contact measurement method, which can effectively solve measurement issues such as unstable flow fields and uneven flow velocity distribution in the pipeline. It supports continuous and stable monitoring in harsh environments with high temperature, high humidity, and high dust during the flue gas discharge process. Suitable for thick-walled chimneys

Properties

  • Suitable for thin-walled chimneys
  • Suitable for acidic and wet gas flows
  • Measurement results are not affected by changes in gas composition.
  • Very low measurement rate with a detection limit of 0.1 m/s.
  • Suitable for chimneys with diameters of 0.3 m to 12 m.
  • Corrosion-resistant materials used in all parts in contact with the gas.
  • Capable of use in corrosive and high-temperature environments.
  • Equipped with a high-speed data processor for faster execution, calibration, and configuration functions.
  • Simultaneous measurement of temperature, pressure, and gas flow rate.
  • Suitable for use in environments with complex flow fields and vortex flows.
