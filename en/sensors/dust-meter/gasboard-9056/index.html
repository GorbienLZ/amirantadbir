Gasboard-9056

Ultra-low laser extractive dust meter: This flue particulate concentration monitoring system can measure flue particle concentration online in harsh environments such as high wind, rain, dust, and extreme temperatures. It consists of three main parts shown in the figure below: Measurement probe (MP): The measurement probe is mounted on the flue to extract gas samples. Processing unit (IPU): This section includes the internal electronic control unit, jet pump, temperature sensor, and laser analysis module. It maintains a constant temperature of the sampled flue gas, controls sample extraction, and measures particle concentration. Fan unit (FU): This section provides clean air at a specified pressure and mass flow rate to the IPU.
