In Situ Dust Meter

In-situ dust meter: This product uses the optical backscatter principle of a red laser to measure flue particulate concentration. A highly stable laser source passes through the flue and illuminates the particulate. The particles scatter the laser, and the intensity of the scattered signal changes proportionally with particulate concentration.
