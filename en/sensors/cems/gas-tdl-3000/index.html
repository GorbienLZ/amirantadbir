GAS TDL-3000

The GAS TDL-3000 gas analyzer uses tunable diode laser absorption spectroscopy (TDLAS) to monitor NH3, HCl, and HF concentrations online. It employs high-temperature trace heating extraction to reduce gas condensation. It provides accurate real-time data on ammonia, HCl, and HF concentration changes, offering reliable environmental monitoring data.

Properties

  • No cross-interference: uses TDLAS with gas-specific laser wavelengths for easy component identification
  • High accuracy: resonance absorption by specific wavelengths is precisely controlled
  • Fast response: quicker than electrochemical, semiconductor infrared, and other methods
  • Very low detection limits
  • Extractive measurement: unaffected by particulate, temperature, or pressure fluctuations
  • Easy installation
  • Low maintenance costs: no difficult repairs required
  • User-friendly calibration and operation
  • No professional operator required
  • Compatible with dusty, high-temperature, high-humidity, and corrosive environments
