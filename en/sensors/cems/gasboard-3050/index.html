Gasboard-3050

The Gasboard-3050 analyzer uses the zirconia ceramic principle to measure oxygen and wet oxygen gas. According to the functional relationships between dry oxygen content, wet oxygen content, and sensor output signal, moisture content and oxygen concentration are calculated and corresponding signals are output, providing a precise basis for flue gas moisture monitoring. The Gasboard-3050 features high accuracy and long service life. It is an oxygen and moisture analyzer ideal for high-temperature and high-humidity environments. It meets the technical requirements for Continuous Emission Monitoring Systems (CEMS).

Properties

  • Simultaneous measurement of O2 and H2O concentrations
  • Detector equipped with heater to prevent water condensation
  • Made of stainless steel with high corrosion resistance, suitable for harsh environments
  • Supports RS-232 and RS-485 communication
